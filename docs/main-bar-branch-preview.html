3 · Status descriptions

Full definition of every program status, grouped by phase.

Authorization

Waiting Authorization
Requested, Blank, Open, Draft, or Deferred — not yet decided.
Approved
Authorization granted; service can proceed.
Denied
Authorization refused; service is stopped/held.

Verification

Pending Verification
A verification request was made.
Verified
Verification completed.

Service

Waiting for Kitchen Assignment
Approved but not yet assigned a kitchen/cadence.
Active
We are providing service.
On Hold
Service paused for all individual members of one household.
Out of Range
Delivery address is outside our delivery range.
Out of Orbit
Member dietary restrictions exceed kitchen capabilities.
Service Expired
The authorization approval window for service expired.
Canceled
Service stopped manually, or the governing case was closed.
Does Not Qualify
Member insurance / social-care coverage is not supported.
Closed
Program closed.
Healthy / active In progress Attention / paused Blocked / stopped Neutral / terminal
